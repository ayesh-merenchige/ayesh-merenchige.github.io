RESEARCH

 

Weight-Lifting Posture Evaluator (LSTM + MediaPipe)

Analyzing motion over time instead of just static poses.

Performance: 94% accuracy (Bicep Curls) · 96% accuracy (Tricep Kickbacks)

- How it works: Tracks the user's body movements across multiple video frames using Google MediaPipe. Instead of judging a single frozen picture, the LSTM network looks at the sequence of motion—this makes it much better at catching bad form.

- Key Features: Extracts simple body metrics from each frame, such as joint angles, torso tilt, and head position, to understand the full movement pattern.

- User Feedback: A lightweight Flask web app gives live, frame-by-frame tips on common mistakes (e.g., flared  elbows, twisting torso, or misaligned wrists).

- Impact: A practical, camera-only solution that helps home fitness users correct their technique and avoid injury without needing expensive sensors.

 

- Stack: Python, TensorFlow, Keras, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, MediaPipe, Flask, HTML/CSS.

 

Lightweight Cashew Nut Grader (Optimized YOLOv8 + Raspberry Pi 4B)

Making a super-accurate model tiny enough to run on cheap hardware.

Performance: 97.21% classification accuracy · 0.949 mAP (detection score) · Runs at 30 FPS on a Raspberry Pi 4B.

 

- New Dataset: Built and labeled the first cashew nut image dataset from Northern Province, Sri Lanka—covering whole, half-split, broken, and diseased nuts.

- The "Small but Accurate" Challenge: To run on a Raspberry Pi, the model must be tiny. We used pruning (removing unnecessary connections) and quantization (compressing the numbers) to shrink YOLOv8 Nano.

- Proof of Performance: We compared our compressed model against larger models like YOLOv8 and FRCNN. The results confirmed our tiny model matched their high accuracy—proving that we didn't sacrifice performance to make it fit on the Pi.

- Live Deployment: Integrated with a conveyor-belt camera for instant, automatic grading. Results are displayed directly on a monitor.

- Impact: A low-cost, automated solution that helps local cashew processors sort nuts faster and with fewer human errors than manual checking.

 

- Stack: Python, YOLOv8, TensorFlow, PyQt for UI, NumPy, Pandas.
